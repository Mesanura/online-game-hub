Welcome to the Sci-Fi Starter Pack!

A careful crafted 16x16 pixel art asset pack designed for 2D platformers, action games, and sci-fi environments. Perfect for creating high-tech research facilities, space stations, and futuristic laboratories with a consistent visual style.

==================================================
		WHAT'S INCLUDED?
==================================================
• Complete Sci-Fi Tileset
  - Wall tiles, floor panels with dark rivet details, inner fills, corners, and neon indicator borders.
• 16 Sci-Fi & Laboratory Props
  - Cryo tanks, workstation computer, biohazard waste bin, lockers, pipes, hanging cables, wall ventilator, and tech props.

==================================================
		  FILE FORMATS
==================================================
• Combined Spritesheet (Tileset + Props combined in transparent PNG)
• Standalone Sheets (Tileset-only & Props-only PNGs)
• Individual Sprites (30+ PNG files neatly categorized in folders)
• Tile size: 16x16 pixels
